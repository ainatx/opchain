# Self-checks (every verb)

Each is a **same-session self-check: the same assistant that wrote the output, applying a checklist; a discipline, not an independent review.** Each reports OK, FINDING or NOT-CHECKED (reason), agent-checked, recorded where the phase file says; fix and re-run while any is FINDING.

| Check | Runs in | Passes when |
|---|---|---|
| No internal numbers | client outputs, `spec/` | By category (below), in every sentence; `spec/` carries no money or payment wording that is not the client's own fact from the plan |
| Trace | `-update` | Every "This week" sentence maps to a commit, builder report, checkpoint row or note |
| No false done | `-update`, `-demo`, `-ready` | Nothing is claimed done without a passing acceptance check. A completion claim (a done word, "now <does>", "no longer", "has been <done>", "has run") traces to a demo record, or to a passing builder report that marks PASS the check the sentence is about, never to a note or commit alone |
| Priced and signed before work | `-change`, `-update`, `-demo` | No change-order work is reported started, handed to the builder or put in `spec/` before a signed amendment. In a sentence about an unsigned change order, or the next one that refers back to it ("it", "this"), only the paperwork (asked, priced, sent, agreed in principle) and the future may appear: no "already", no "we have <done>", no first draft, groundwork or half-built, no work continuing |
| Ledger arithmetic | `-start`, `-invoice` | Amounts match the quote and signed change orders: tool output recorded, else `not machine-checked` |
| No private names | every output | No private skill, no business, not the builder's name or say: lines |

**The categories of an internal number.** "However worded" means by what a sentence says, not by one phrase. A client output carries none of these: **a quantity of time or effort** (any number, in figures or words, of minutes, hours, days, evenings, weekends, weeks, fortnights or months, "a couple of days", "most of last weekend"); **an amount of money** that is not a client-facing figure (the fixed price, a payment, a change order's price, the contract total) — never "2k", "grand" or an amount in words; **a ratio**: a multiplier ("1.25×", "one and a quarter times", "doubled"), a fraction of a price ("a quarter on top", "a fifth") or a percentage outside the payment schedule; **our side of the money**: what the work costs us against what we charged, a profit or a loss, our rates or estimates; **an overrun** against our own plan, with or without a number. The exempt fields are where they sit: the payment terms ("due within 14 days"), a change order's Schedule, a date that moves, the payment schedule's percentages.

**What a checker cannot catch.** A done claim with no marker at all ("Your invoices match the orders exactly") reads as a plain statement; before you write any *This week* sentence about the client's system, ask whether it says something now works, and if so trace it as a done claim.
