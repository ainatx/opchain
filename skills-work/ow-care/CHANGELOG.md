Implements the approved `docs/plans/2026-10-16-ow-care-design.md`: frozen terms, two entry paths, nine operating verbs, monthly health and hours/reports, approvals, changes, notice and access-aware ending. An acceptance checker with golden runs, plants and held-out sets lands with this entry; those are test and data edits.
