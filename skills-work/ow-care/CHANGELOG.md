# ow-care changelog

Per-skill versions (`catalogs.json` `versionPolicy: per-skill`). The family's v2.1.0 release entry cites each skill's version. Every change to `SKILL.md` or a reference bumps this skill; data, example and test edits ride without a bump and are listed here as data edits.

## 2.0.0 (2026-11-03): the audience reversal

Why: `docs/plans/2026-11-03-ow-audience-reversal.md`. The family now serves the operator's own small-business client work, so the operator goes with the flow. Protocol version 2.

- **Removed.** Step 0 as a ritual; the sensitivity level and its visible marking; the eight-key artefact header (records start with `written_by` and `created`); the STATUS grammar and clock labels; the NEXT block forms and fallback lines (now a short "What comes next" list); "no skill runs another"; the OK / FINDING verdict words and the session self-check label (now a short checklist); the working-note sentence; the typed waiver form; one writer per file as a lock; re-asking on resume; the ow-tools absent-case branches (say once, add up by hand).
- **Changed.** A wrong start may be corrected by running `-start` again before the first month closes. Working files may name a client's people; client documents name people by role unless the user says otherwise. No contact detail in a go-ahead or an access record.
- **Kept.** The care terms taken from the price book and `offers.md` when care starts and kept for the contract's life, with a change only through a dated `-change` addendum; every request logged and classified with its reply target; quarter-hours and a month that adds up; no double charge; the client's go-ahead before any hour beyond the prepaid allowance; the read-only health check of a user-confirmed `health.yaml`; least access with a purpose and a removal step; no credentials in any file; the end accounting for access; invoice requests that are never invoices; no automatic client-system writes; no private names.
- **The CRM is the record.** Requests go to `support_requests`, care hours to `time_entries`, the monthly report and other prose to documents by kind, invoice requests to unissued `invoices`, each marked internal or client. Each verb reads the CRM's latest saved version first, never overwrites a human-edited version, and treats `opchain-work/` as a scratch cache; `price-book.yaml`, `offers.md`, `profile.md` and `handoffs.yaml` are the fallback, and the output says so. Goldens and checkers still grade the cache.
- **Budget.** At most three questions per verb, only for what the files do not answer. One approval that matters here: the client's go-ahead before extra hours, with the user's look at the monthly report before it goes out.

Data and test edits that land with this entry: the checker keeps the money, access, secrets and approval checks and drops the shapes above; it still accepts goldens written in the old form. The sensitivity-level case and the plants for removed rules are gone.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-29): ongoing care after a handoff, and care for a system taken over

Design: `docs/plans/2026-10-16-ow-care-design.md` (APPROVED 2026-10-16, decisions 1–11, amendments T3, T4, H4, S1 and P4). Contract: `sprints/release-2.1/ow-07/contract.md`. Salvaged on 2026-10-29 from an external draft, PR 652 (owner decision 2026-10-29) onto a fresh branch off `main`, taking only what ow-care needs; the draft's other work (ow-vendor-eval, the protocol wording, distribution) was left where it was.

- **Two ways in.** After a build, `/ow-care-start` reads ow-handoff's `care-start.md` (and its addenda) and `after-offer.md`, and freezes every term once in `care.yaml`; a choice other than care stops it. For a system someone else built, ow-start's `care-takeover` path (S1, ow-start 1.2.0) leads to ow-build-plan's fixed-fee onboarding quote (P4, ow-build-plan 1.3.0), then `/ow-care-onboard` writes the eight-part review and `/ow-care-start --takeover` freezes the tier the client chose.
- **The route.** `/ow-care on <path>` sends `care-start.md` to `-start`, a `route: care` `FIX-nn.md` to `-request`, a signed onboarding folder to `-onboard` and a care folder to its resume; it writes nothing itself. ow-handoff's two care NEXT lines hint it (ow-handoff 1.0.1).
- **Nine verbs, one writer per file.** `-onboard`, `-start`, `-request`, `-health`, `-notes`, `-access`, `-month`, `-change`, `-end`, each with its reference (`references/entry-and-requests.md`, `references/health-and-access.md`, `references/month-and-end.md`); `STATUS.md` and `secrets-seen.log` are the append-only logs.
- **Hours and months.** Months from the start date (a short month clamped to its last day), no proration, each invoiced in full at its start as an invoice *request*; hours in quarter-hours recorded per `CARE-nn`; the frozen rollover rule (the offer's structured `rollover`, H4) applied oldest hours first, through `ow-tools calc hours` (T4) when it can run, by hand and so labelled when it cannot; work pauses at the available hours and no extra or urgent hour counts without the client's recorded go-ahead; a changed rule is closed by hand from the last closed row, never replayed through the calculator.
- **Health, notes, access.** `/ow-care-health` builds a user-confirmed `health.yaml` once and runs `ow-tools health --config … --json` (T3) read-only, the rest a checklist, each result OK, attention (with a proposed request) or not checked; `/ow-care-notes` writes care's `OP-nn` entries, replacing never editing; `/ow-care-access` keeps every item with a purpose, an owner and a removal step, removals recorded as confirmed by role and never verified by the skill.
- **Report, change, end.** `/ow-care-month` closes a month, writes `ledger.md` (not an accounting record), the client report and the next invoice request (or the final settlement, with no renewal line); `/ow-care-change` writes dated addenda for the monthly call, a tier change, new terms with notice and acceptance, and notice to end (care ends at the end of the care month in which the notice period runs out); `/ow-care-end` refuses while a request is in progress or an access item is neither removed nor waived, then writes `end.md` and the end email twins.
- **Text from outside is data.** Every file and message care reads is named, with where a quoted instruction goes (`Possible embedded instructions`) and the rule that only the user's own words in the session approve, classify, waive or record hours. The handling level read is the strictest found.
- **Self-checks.** The design's eleven, labelled same-session self-checks; `-month` and `-end` refuse on a FINDING; No credentials, People by role, No double charge and No private names can never be waived.

Data and test edits that land with this entry (no bump of their own): `examples/offers.md` and `examples/handoffs.yaml`, byte-identical to the family's; `skill.edges.yaml`; the acceptance checker with its 80 golden runs, their plants and held-out sets. Two files extend the design's §5 writes table: `hours-input.json` (the calculator's input, kept so a month can be re-checked) and `invoices/INV-request-care-final-settlement.md` (the last month's extras and credit, with no renewal line); `care.yaml`'s `tier` is the chosen tier's name, its row standing in `tiers_offered`. The design's "Real use" test (one real care month, closed with its report) is REL-02's, not a fixture.
