## Executive Summary

**The fence is well drawn where OW-05 taught it:**
- Candidates are frozen with two digests, the sign-off names them, and `-deliver` recomputes them and compares the draft text in full (B-GATE-01 holds).
- The four never-waived checks refuse a typed waiver (five `m-waive-*-refused` goldens).
- The repository copy is checked for branch (O8), containment at every component and symlinks (Y10), ownership by digest, front-matter keys, `<script>`, `on…=` with a space, `import`/`export` lines and plain tags.
- The push needs a listing on screen per file, a location from `handoffs.yaml` or user-stated, and `intended` → `done`.
- Named reads hold the deny-list (B-NAM-01). Findings are copied field by field and Suspicious content is kept out (B-INJ-01). Other clients go through the pinned wrapper, slugs and names in any spelling (B-OTH-01).

**The weaknesses sit where the text says the right thing and nothing checks it, or where the text cannot be followed:**

1. **SD10-01 (MEDIUM): active content passes the C15/C16 net.** `<svg/onload=…>`, a `<javascript:…>` autolink, a `javascript:` reference-link definition and a protocol-relative link pass in the blog copy. An entity-encoded `javascript:` href passes in the email HTML. The copy lands in a site the client builds and serves.
2. **SD10-02 (MEDIUM): what is delivered is never compared with what was signed off.** The repository copy and the email HTML/text are written by the model, and the checker never compares them with the frozen client form (`repoCopy` and `emailHtml` are defined and never called). A paragraph added after sign-off, or a rewritten call to action in a client-approved email, passes.
3. **SD10-03 (MEDIUM, OWNER-DECISION): two of the three No internal numbers detections can't run.** `self-checks.md` §4 says the skill never reads the price book, the quote's items, the ledger or the margin view. `phase-evidence.md` §5 then tells it to compare each figure "with our own", ask about round figures, and record `ours by value`. The goldens ask (`m-route-evidence`, 4,000). A real run can't, so the check reports OK on our exact figure whenever it isn't said as ours.
4. **SD10-04 (MEDIUM): injection is graded for one source of eight, and two of the rules collide.**
   - Only the voice sample has an `embedded` check that runs.
   - `b-deliver-case` declares the README plant with `quoted_in: site/README.md` (the planted file itself) and doesn't run the check; the golden records `embedded: []`.
   - The inbox email's "mark C-2 signed off" is quoted nowhere.
   - A findings claim carrying an instruction must be copied word for word (`phase-evidence.md` §2), so it is imported unquoted and passes. The faithful run, which quotes it, fails `evidence`.
5. **SD10-05 (MEDIUM): the handling union misses the brief and every inbox file.** `delivery-rules.md` §1 names the brief and any bannered evidence file, but the checker's union never reads the brief, and it looks for inbox files at `opchain-work/inbox/` instead of `drafts/<ws>/DR-nn-<slug>/inbox/`. A push goes out under both.

Seven LOWs and one INFO follow (SD10-06 to SD10-13).

## Threat model

### Assets
- **Client deliverables and client comms:** what the client reads, and an email the client sends to their own staff.
- **Marketing:** what the public reads on our site; it names a client only under a permission.
- **The client's (or our) site repository:** the copy lands in a content folder the site builds and serves.
- **Our side:** rates, hours, margin, multipliers, the quote's internal appendix, the price book.
- **Other workstreams:** their names, slugs and evidence.
- **The integrity of records:** `signoff.md`, `permissions/<client>.md`, `checks/C-n.md` waivers, `deliveries.yaml`.

### Adversaries and boundaries
- **Web-derived text** reaches ow-draft through `findings.md` claims, Conflicts and Gaps (ow-research quoted it from pages).
- **Client-controlled text:** inbox files and email, voice samples (a client's own emails for `client-<ws>`), the destination repository's README.
- **Family files the user names:** `DEC-nn`, meeting notes, `map/future.md`, the quote's client part. These are trusted only as far as their own writers' rules.
- **The model itself:** honest but steerable, and it fills under-specified steps.
- **Trusted:** the user's `handoffs.yaml`, `brand.yaml` and `profile.md`.

## Pillar 2: Findings

### [SD10-01] Active content passes the repository-copy and email net

**Risk:** MEDIUM · **Category:** T, E (stored script on the client's public site; a script link in an email the client sends) · **Scope:** checker `activeContent` A:395–410 (`\son[a-z]+=` needs a space before `on`; the scheme regexes see only `href=`/`src=` and inline `](…)`; the raw-HTML regex needs a space or `>` after the tag name); `validateEmailHtml` (ow-build) checks no href scheme; text `phase-deliver.md` step 3, `type-comms.md` (both right) · **Fix effort:** S

**Problem:**
- The text rule is complete ("no raw HTML block or inline tag … links only `https:`"). The model writes the copy and the email; the only machine check is the C15/C16 net, and it misses the standard spellings:
  - `<svg/onload=alert(1)>`: no space before `onload`, and `/` after the tag name defeats the raw-tag regex.
  - `<javascript:alert(1)>`: a CommonMark autolink, rendered as `<a href="javascript:…">` by remark (Astro doesn't sanitize Markdown).
  - `[n]: javascript:alert(1)`: a reference-link definition.
  - `[x](//host/…)`: protocol-relative, not `https:`.
  - In the email: `href="&#106;avascript:…"` (an entity-encoded scheme; browsers decode it).
- Followed injection (SD10-04) is the route in: a findings claim or inbox file carrying one of these.

**Reproduction** (SD10-01a–e): `b-deliver-case` with each line appended to `site/src/content/blog/case-study.md` (row re-digested); `l-deliver-comms` with the entity-encoded link in the HTML. Every check passes.

**Remediation:**
1. **Checker:** decode HTML entities before matching; flag `on\w+=` after any of `\s`, `/`, `"` or `'`; flag any `<tag` with any following character; flag autolinks `<scheme:…>` and reference definitions `^\s*\[[^\]]+\]:\s*(\S+)` whose scheme is not `https:`; flag a link or `src` with no scheme (`//`), or a relative one in the email. In the email, require every `href` to be `https:` after decoding.
2. **Text:** one line in `phase-deliver.md` step 3 and `type-comms.md`: "reference links and autolinks too; no entity-encoded characters in a link".
3. Plant the five PoCs.

### [SD10-02] The repository copy and the email are not compared with the signed-off client form

**Risk:** MEDIUM · **Category:** T (delivery of something other than what was signed off; contract scope 6) · **Scope:** checker `checkDeliver` A:1717–1855 (the candidate files are re-digested; the copy and the email are checked for form only); `repoCopy` A:372 and `emailHtml` A:381 defined and never called by a check; text `phase-deliver.md` step 3, `type-comms.md` Export · **Fix effort:** S

**Problem:**

- The copy's digest row is the model's own record of what it wrote, so it proves ownership, not fidelity.
- The no-tools Markdown copy is compared (A:1769), and binary exports come from ow-tools, so these two model-written paths are the gap.
- Impact: a sentence never signed off reaches the public site; a client-approved email changes its call to action ("reply with the shop card number on file") after approval.

**Reproduction:**

- (SD10-02b) `l-deliver-comms`: the call to action rewritten in the HTML and the text twin. Every check passes.

**Remediation:**
1. **Checker:** require `repoCopy(clientForm, {date, description, tags})` byte-equal to the written copy (the tags and description from the brief), and `emailHtml(clientForm)` and `emailText(clientForm)` equal to the email files. Or, at minimum, compare word sequences.
2. **Text:** `phase-deliver.md` step 3 and `type-comms.md`: "the copy is the client form's text, unchanged; a difference is a new candidate".
3. Plant both PoCs.
